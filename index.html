RACE & ETHNIC POLITICS

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at the University of Michigan. My research examines Black political behavior, political psychology, and the ways Black Americans make sense of and respond to persistent racial inequality within American democracy.

My work uses quantitative and qualitative methods to study political attitudes, identity, democratic engagement, and the psychological consequences of racialized political experiences.
