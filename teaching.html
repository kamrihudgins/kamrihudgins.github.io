TEACHING

Teaching Philosophy

My approach to teaching centers around three core principles: inclusion, empowerment, and creativity. I aspire to create a classroom environment where students are inspired to ask difficult questions and take intellectual ownership over their learning. With an ultimate goal of connecting the study of political science to the world they navigate in their everyday life.
